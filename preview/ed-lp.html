Important Safety Information

Sildenafil and tadalafil are prescription PDE5 inhibitors. Common side effects include headache, flushing, nasal congestion, back pain, and indigestion. Do not take with nitrates or alpha-blockers. Seek immediate medical attention for erections lasting more than 4 hours (priapism) or for sudden vision or hearing changes. Oxytocin is included in this compounded formula at a strength determined by your provider. Tell your provider about every medication and medical condition you have before starting. Compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety or effectiveness. Always consult your healthcare provider.
