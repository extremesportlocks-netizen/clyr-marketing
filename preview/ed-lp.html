Important Safety Information

Tadalafil and Vardenafil are prescription PDE5 inhibitors. Common side effects include headache, flushing, nasal congestion, back pain, and indigestion. Do not take with nitrates or alpha-blockers. Seek immediate medical attention for erections lasting more than 4 hours (priapism) or sudden vision or hearing changes. Compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety or effectiveness. Always consult your healthcare provider.
