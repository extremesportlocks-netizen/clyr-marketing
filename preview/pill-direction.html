The tablet itself is the ad.

Hims sells a small mint-format tablet with a brand deboss. Ours currently reads like a spec sheet pressed into a pill. Same formula, two directions below.

Current

T3 ODT oval

Large oval, spec text on the face. Reads clinical. The deboss says what it is, not who made it.

Proposed

CLYR oval, refined

Same oval, cleaner execution. Bevel ring, soft sheen, and CLYR debossed on the face instead of spec text. Live on the preview LP now.

Why this matters now

  • The tablet is still in formulation with TPH. Shape, color, and deboss can be specified before the first pressing, at which point the render and the real product match.
  • Ask Talaj and Aarti what ODT punch and die shapes their tooling supports, whether a custom CLYR deboss die is possible, and lead time plus minimums for either.
  • If custom tooling is a no, the fallback is their closest small round or triangle punch with a CLYR deboss, and this concept render adjusts to match.
  • Marketing render ships to the LP only after the physical spec is locked, so the page never shows a tablet a patient will not receive.
