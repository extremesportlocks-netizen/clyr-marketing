Important Safety Information

Tadalafil, Vardenafil, and Sildenafil are prescription PDE5 inhibitors. Common side effects include headache, flushing, nasal congestion, back pain, and indigestion. Do not take with nitrates or alpha-blockers. Seek immediate medical attention for erections lasting more than 4 hours (priapism) or sudden vision or hearing changes. PT-141 (bremelanotide) may cause nausea, flushing, and transient increases in blood pressure. Compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies and have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety or effectiveness. Always consult your healthcare provider.
